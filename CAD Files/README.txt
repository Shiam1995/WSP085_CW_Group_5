Zipline International Inc. is an American company that designs, manufactures, and operates delivery drones, 
with distribution centers with locations:
United States,Rwanda , Ghana,Japan,Nigeria, Côte d'Ivoire, and Kenya. 
Its drones deliver blood products, vaccines, and medical supplies, and the company also operates 
services for consumer and retail deliveries.
